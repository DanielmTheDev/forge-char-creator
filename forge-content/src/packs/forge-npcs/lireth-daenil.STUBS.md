# Lireth Daenil — what is NOT automated

Both actions are gate-proven (Emberlight authored new for this actor; tally-stake
is an example-strike reskin at 1d6). Statblock pieces deliberately left manual:

- **Tally-stake snap-off** — "can snap off in wound; next save vs it disadv."
  Conditional rider on a hit; kept as a biography note for the DM to adjudicate.
- **Emberlight area light** — "lights a dark area for 1 round." Token light
  toggle / GM flavor, not an actor mechanic.
- **Fey Ancestry** — advantage vs. charm + sleep immunity. Situational; midi
  conditional flags are brittle here. Biography note.
- **Keen Senses** — modeled as real Perception proficiency (skills.prc) +
  darkvision 60; the "advantage to spot hidden" wording is DM-side.
- **atk +4 / DC 13** — derived: STR 14 (+2) + assumed prof +2 = +4 melee;
  Emberlight DC forced to flat 13 (custom calculation), independent of stats.
  Ability scores are INVENTED (statblock gave none): 14/14/12/10/12/16.
